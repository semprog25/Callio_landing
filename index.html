Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Callio

What is Callio? +
Callio is your missed-call rescue assistant. When an unknown number calls and you miss it, Callio helps you find out why — before you decide whether to call back.
Does Callio send messages automatically? +
No. Callio prepares a short rescue message, but you always review and confirm before anything is sent through your phone’s SMS composer.
Do you upload my call logs? +
No. Callio is designed to keep call context on your device. We do not build a central database of phone numbers.
What permissions does Callio need? +
To detect missed calls and unknown numbers, Callio may request call log, phone state, and contacts access. SMS is sent through the system composer — Callio is not a default SMS app.
Is there a paid plan? +
The MVP is free while we learn what people need. Callio Plus may later unlock advanced summarization and smart reminders — without gating the core rescue flow during testing.
Can I delete my data? +
Yes. You can delete individual calls or wipe all Callio data from Settings at any time.
When is Callio available? +
Callio is coming to Google Play. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it’s ready. Android first; iOS may follow based on demand.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

Last Updated: January 01, 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Callio, your privacy is our top priority. Callio is a missed-call rescue assistant. We designed it to keep your call context on your device whenever possible.

Data Collection

Your calls stay yours. Callio stores rescue context on-device by default. We do not sell call data and we do not build a central caller database.

No Login or Account Required

You don't need to create an account or provide any personal information to use Callio. No email, no phone number, no tracking. Just install and use.

Local Storage

Missed-call records, notes, and rescue messages are stored locally on your device. You can delete individual calls or wipe all Callio data anytime from Settings.

No Third-Party Sharing

We never share your data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Analytics companies
  • Data brokers
  • Any other third parties

Your call data is not for sale. You control every rescue message.

Monetization

The Callio Rescue MVP is free while we learn what people need. If we introduce optional ads or paid upgrades later, we will not sell your call data, and you will still control every rescue message.

Permissions

Callio may request certain Android permissions to function:

  • Phone / call logs / phone state: To detect missed calls from unknown numbers
  • Contacts: To distinguish known contacts from unknown callers (data stays on your device)

Rescue SMS is sent through your phone’s system composer. Callio is not a default SMS app. Permissions are used for app functionality; call context is kept on-device by default.

Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technologies. We have no idea how you use the app, and that's by design.

Children's Privacy

Callio does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
